# Business Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the business principles that guide all business decisions, product decisions, prioritization activities, and investment decisions within AI Studio projects.

This document serves as the source of truth for:

```text
Business Enablement Agent
Product Owner Agent
UI/UX Designer Agent
Architect Agent
Reviewer Agent
Project Stakeholders
```

---

# Core Philosophy

Every initiative must answer:

```text
Why are we building this?

Who benefits from this?

How is success measured?

What business value will be created?
```

Building software is not the objective.

Creating business value is the objective.

---

# Business First Principle

Business outcomes come before technology choices.

---

## Correct Approach

```text
Business Problem
        ↓
Business Goals
        ↓
Requirements
        ↓
Design
        ↓
Implementation
```

---

## Incorrect Approach

```text
Technology Choice
        ↓
Features
        ↓
Business Justification
```

Technology must support business goals.

Business goals must never be created merely to justify technology.

# Problem Before Solution

Always understand the problem before proposing solutions.

---

## Required Analysis

Identify:

```text
Current State

Desired State

Business Gap

Root Cause
```

---

## Never Assume

Avoid:

```text
The requested feature is the correct solution.

The first idea is the best idea.

More functionality creates more value.
```

---

# Root Cause Thinking

Every problem should be investigated beyond symptoms.

---

## Example

Problem:

```text
Customer creation takes too long.
```

Possible Root Causes:

```text
Too many approval steps

Poor user interface

Manual validation

Duplicate entry process
```

Never solve symptoms without understanding root causes.

# Prioritization Principles

Not everything should be built.

Prioritize based on value.

---

# MoSCoW Framework

## Must Have

Critical requirements.

Without these the solution fails.

Examples:

```text
Authentication

Core Business Functions

Mandatory Compliance Requirements
```

---

## Should Have

Important but not mission critical.

Examples:

```text
Advanced Search

Extended Filtering
```

---

## Could Have

Useful enhancements.

Examples:

```text
Export Features

Optional Reports
```

---

## Won't Have

Items intentionally excluded from current scope.

Examples:

```text
Future Features

Optional Enhancements
```

---

# Value vs Effort Principle

Prioritize:

```text
High Value
Low Effort
```

First.

---

## Priority Matrix

### High Value + Low Effort

Implement immediately.

---

### High Value + High Effort

Plan carefully.

---

### Low Value + Low Effort

Implement if capacity exists.

---

### Low Value + High Effort

Avoid unless justified.

---

# MVP Principle

Deliver the smallest solution that produces business value.

---

## MVP Goals

Provide:

```text
Fast Validation

Quick Feedback

Reduced Risk

Reduced Cost
```

---

## Avoid

```text
Over Engineering

Building Everything At Once

Premature Optimization
```

---

# Incremental Delivery Principle

Deliver value continuously.

Avoid massive releases.

Preferred:

```text

↓

Feedback

↓

Phase 2

↓

Feedback

↓
```

```text

Expected Growth
```

---

# Definition Of Business Success

An initiative is successful when:

```text
Business Goals Achieved

KPIs Met

Users Adopt Solution

Risks Managed

Stakeholders Satisfied

Sustainable Operations Established
```

---

# Definition Of Failure

An initiative is considered unsuccessful when:

```text
No Clear Business Value

Low Adoption

Unmeasurable Outcomes

Excessive Cost

Uncontrolled Risk

Misalignment With Business Goals
```

---

# Decision Framework

Before recommending implementation verify:

```text
Problem Clearly Defined

Root Cause Understood

Stakeholders Identified

Business Value Defined

Success Metrics Defined

Scope Defined

Risks Evaluated

Priority Assigned
```

---

# Golden Rule

Always ask:

```text
Why should we build this?
```

Before asking:

```text
How should we build this?
```

Business value always comes before implementation.
